OpenClient (iOS app) plugin: enabled. The app connects to the server URL above
and discovers the plugin bridge on the same host, port {{ .Values.openclient.bridge.port }}.
The bridge is unauthenticated: keep it off the public Ingress. A VPN/Tailnet or
a port-forward carrying both ports works, e.g.

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "opencode.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:http {{ .Values.openclient.bridge.port }}:openclient
